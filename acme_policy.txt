ACME Corp Employee Policy Handbook
====================================

LEAVE POLICY
------------
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Leave must be approved by your direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.
Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next calendar year.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave and capped at 10 days per year.
Employees on probation (first 3 months) are not eligible for paid leave.

REMOTE WORK POLICY
------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote work must be approved by your team lead on a monthly basis.
Employees are expected to be online and reachable between 10am and 4pm in their local timezone.
All remote work must be done from a secure, private network. Public Wi-Fi is not permitted.
Equipment such as laptops and monitors can be requested from the IT department for home use.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
---------------------
Business expenses up to $100 can be reimbursed without prior approval.
Expenses above $100 require manager sign-off before the purchase is made.
All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
Receipts are mandatory for all claims above $20.
Travel expenses for client meetings are fully covered if booked through the company travel portal.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
-------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.
Employees set goals at the start of each review cycle in collaboration with their manager.
Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5, where 3 is "meets expectations".
Employees rated 4 or above for two consecutive cycles are eligible for a promotion review.
Underperformance (rating of 1 or 2) triggers a 60-day improvement plan.

IT AND SECURITY
---------------
All employees must complete a cybersecurity training course within their first month.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
Sharing login credentials with colleagues is strictly prohibited.
Any suspected security breach must be reported to it-security@acme.com within 1 hour of discovery.
Personal devices used for work must have company-approved antivirus software installed.
